Fractional Sales Management for Contractors

Fractional sales management is a model where an experienced sales leader works with a contracting company on a part-time basis, providing the strategic direction, team coaching, process development, and accountability that a full-time VP of Sales would deliver. Instead of hiring a $150,000-plus executive who may or may not understand the construction industry, contractors engage a fractional leader for a defined number of hours each week, typically three to four, and receive hands-on sales leadership from someone who has already built and managed sales organizations in the trades.

For contracting companies between $5 million and $50 million in revenue, fractional sales management solves a problem that training alone cannot: the gap between knowing what to do and having someone who ensures it gets done. Most contractors do not lack sales knowledge. They lack a leader who runs the meetings, coaches the reps, tracks the metrics, and holds the team accountable to a defined process every single week. Sales Transformation Group has provided fractional sales leadership to contractors since 2017, working with over 1,000 companies across roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and general contracting.

Why Contractors Need Fractional Sales Leadership

The typical path for a growing contracting company follows a predictable pattern. The owner starts as the sole salesperson, closing deals on relationships and reputation. As the company grows, the owner hires one or two reps but continues to manage sales informally because there is no one else to do it. The reps receive minimal training, attend no structured meetings, and operate with no consistent process. By the time the company reaches $3 million to $10 million in revenue, the owner is stretched across operations, estimating, hiring, and sales management simultaneously. Something has to give, and it is usually the sales management that suffers first.

The Owner Cannot Be the Sales Manager Forever

Sales Transformation Group's assessments of over 1,000 contractors reveal that the most common bottleneck to growth is not lead generation or market demand. It is the absence of dedicated sales leadership. The owner who built the company on personal selling ability cannot scale that ability by hiring reps and hoping they figure it out. Reps need weekly coaching, pipeline reviews, accountability to KPIs, and someone who helps them navigate complex deals in real time. Without that structure, even talented salespeople underperform because no one is managing the process. Damschroder Roofing spent years stuck at $5 million with this exact dynamic. It was not until they implemented STG's structured framework with dedicated leadership cadence that they broke through to $10.1 million and set course for $20 million.

Hiring a Full-Time VP of Sales Is Risky and Expensive

The alternative to fractional management is hiring a full-time VP of Sales. In the construction industry, a qualified sales executive commands a base salary of $150,000 or more per year, plus commissions, benefits, and equity expectations. Beyond the cost, the risk is significant. Finding someone who understands both sales leadership and the contracting world is difficult. Many companies hire a great salesperson and assume they will be a great manager, which rarely works. Others recruit from outside the industry and spend a year watching the new hire learn the business on the company's dime. A bad VP of Sales hire can cost $200,000 to $300,000 in direct compensation plus the opportunity cost of a year of stalled growth. Fractional management eliminates this risk by providing a proven leader with contractor-specific expertise from day one.

What Fractional Sales Management Includes

Sales Transformation Group's Platinum Fractional Sales Leadership program delivers a defined set of services on a weekly and monthly cadence. This is not advisory. This is operational sales leadership embedded in your company.

Weekly Sales Meeting Facilitation

The fractional sales leader runs your weekly sales team meeting with a structured agenda that includes pipeline review, deal strategy, activity metrics, and accountability to commitments made the previous week. Most contracting companies either do not hold regular sales meetings or run meetings that are unfocused status updates with no coaching component. STG's meeting structure follows the Baseline Selling methodology and ensures every meeting produces actionable outcomes. After working with over 1,000 contractors, STG has refined the meeting format to fit the rhythm of contracting companies where reps are in the field most of the week and meeting time is limited.

One-on-One Rep Coaching

Individual coaching sessions give each rep dedicated time with the fractional leader to review their pipeline, practice skills, strategize on specific deals, and track their progress against STG's 36-milestone journey. This is where the most significant development happens because coaching is personalized to the rep's specific strengths and gaps. Rackley Roofing grew their sales team from 3 to 8 reps over three and a half years of weekly coaching, and VP of Sales Michael Miller credited the individual development with dramatically improving close ratios across the team through better prospect qualification.

Monthly On-Site Visits

The fractional leader visits your office monthly for in-person coaching, ride-alongs with reps, and direct observation of how the sales process plays out in the field. On-site visits build trust with the team, surface issues that do not come through on virtual calls, and provide the hands-on presence that reinforces the importance of the sales process. National Roofing Partners COO Tony Rader described the embedded coaching model as instrumental in exceeding their profit targets because the on-site presence kept the team aligned and accountable.

Sales Infrastructure Development

Beyond weekly management, the fractional leader builds the systems that create lasting change: KPI dashboards, sales playbooks, CRM configuration, hiring criteria for new reps, onboarding processes, and compensation structures. This infrastructure is what separates fractional sales management from training. Training teaches skills. Fractional management builds the organization. When the engagement eventually evolves, the systems remain. STG's 36-milestone journey provides the roadmap for this infrastructure development, ensuring that nothing gets skipped and progress is measurable at every stage.

Hiring and Talent Assessment

The fractional leader assists with vetting and hiring new salespeople, using competency assessments and structured interviews to identify candidates who fit your company's culture and sales model. National Roofing Partners uses STG to assist with hiring and vetting new salespeople, ensuring that every addition to the team meets the standards established by the sales process. Hiring is one of the highest-risk activities in a growing contracting company, and having an experienced sales leader involved in the process reduces the likelihood of expensive mis-hires.
