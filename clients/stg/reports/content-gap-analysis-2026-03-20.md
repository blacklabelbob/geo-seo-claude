# STG Content Gap Analysis: Blue Ocean Opportunities in Roofing/Contractor Sales Training
**Date:** 2026-03-20
**Methodology:** Bain-style competitive gap analysis across 50+ search queries
**Analyst:** Max (AI Strategy Consultant)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

After analyzing 50+ queries across Reddit, Quora, Google SERPs, and industry forums, I identified **20 blue ocean content opportunities** where either (a) no good answer exists, (b) the existing content is generic/shallow, or (c) no competitor owns the topic from a sales leadership perspective. The biggest theme: **everybody teaches reps how to sell roofs, but almost nobody teaches owners how to build, manage, and scale a sales organization.** That is STG's moat.

## THE 20 BLUE OCEAN OPPORTUNITIES

### TIER 1: NO GOOD ANSWER EXISTS (Highest Priority)

---

#### 1. "When Should a Roofing Company Hire Its First Sales Manager?"
**Current SERP:** All results are about hiring sales REPS, not managers. Zero content addresses the specific revenue threshold, team size, or organizational signals that indicate it's time to hire a dedicated sales manager.
- **Who ranks:** IKO, JobNimbus, ServiceTitan, Roof Sales Mastery — all on hiring reps
- **Content quality:** NO relevant content exists for this specific query
- **STG Opportunity:** HIGH
- **Recommended content:** "The $2M Inflection Point: When (and How) to Hire Your First Roofing Sales Manager" — Framework with revenue thresholds, org chart evolution, job description template, compensation benchmarks, and the 5 signs you've outgrown owner-led sales.

---

#### 2. "Roofing Sales Revenue Per Rep Benchmarks"
**Current SERP:** Only Hook Agency mentions $340K revenue per employee (not per sales rep specifically). No source provides a clear benchmark table for revenue per salesperson by company size, market type, or business model.
- **Who ranks:** Hook Agency, ProLine, Allied Emergency Services — fragments only
- **Content quality:** Scattered data points, no comprehensive benchmark guide
- **STG Opportunity:** HIGH
- **Recommended content:** "How Much Should Each Roofing Sales Rep Generate? Revenue-Per-Rep Benchmarks by Company Size" — Include benchmarks for storm vs. retail, residential vs. commercial, year 1 vs. year 3 reps, with a downloadable calculator.

---

#### 3. "Roofing Sales Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Template"
**Current SERP:** Generic sales PIP templates from HR sites (Smartsheet, AIHR, SiftHub). Zero roofing-specific PIP content exists anywhere.
- **Who ranks:** Generic HR/sales enablement sites only
- **Content quality:** Nothing industry-specific
- **STG Opportunity:** HIGH
- **Recommended content:** "The Roofing Sales PIP: How to Save an Underperforming Rep (Or Let Them Go)" — Roofing-specific PIP template with KPI thresholds (close rate, avg ticket, inspections/week), 30-60-90 day milestones, coaching intervention protocols, and when to cut losses.

#### 4. "Roofing Sales Accountability Scorecard Template"

- **STG Opportunity:** HIGH
- **Recommended content:** "The Weekly Sales Scorecard: Hold Your Roofing Reps Accountable Without Micromanaging" — Downloadable template with leading indicators (doors knocked, inspections booked, proposals sent) + lagging indicators (close rate, avg ticket, revenue), with instructions on how to run a 15-min accountability check-in.

#### 5. "How Many Salespeople Should a Roofing Company Have?"
**Current SERP:** No direct answer. Results discuss hiring tactics and compensation but never address the ratio of salespeople to revenue, production capacity, or lead volume.

- **STG Opportunity:** HIGH
- **Recommended content:** "The Sales Team Sizing Formula: How Many Reps Does Your Roofing Company Actually Need?" — Framework based on: lead volume / close rate = required proposals, required proposals / rep capacity = number of reps needed. Include production capacity constraints and seasonal adjustment models.

## STRATEGIC THEMES: WHERE STG WINS

### Theme 1: The Sales LEADERSHIP Gap
Every competitor teaches reps how to sell. Nobody teaches owners how to LEAD a sales organization. STG should own:
- Sales manager hiring, development, and daily operations
- Owner-to-manager transitions
- Sales team structure at every revenue stage

### Theme 2: The "Boring Operations" That Print Money
Templates, SOPs, scorecards, and checklists are the content that gets bookmarked, downloaded, and shared. They also generate leads. STG should create:
- Downloadable templates for every management activity
- Calculator tools (revenue per rep, team sizing, comp plan modeler)
- Checklists that become the industry standard

### Theme 3: The Scale-Up Playbook
Most roofing content serves two audiences: brand new reps and massive enterprises. The $1M-$10M growth corridor is underserved. STG should own:
- Stage-by-stage org evolution guides
- Revenue milestone frameworks
- The operational inflection points most owners miss

### Theme 4: Data-Driven Decision Making
Roofing owners are hungry for benchmarks but can't find them. STG should become the definitive source for:
- Conversion rate benchmarks by source and stage
- Revenue per rep benchmarks
- Compensation benchmarks
- Team sizing formulas
