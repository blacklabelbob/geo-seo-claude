# STG Keyword Universe Audit — Complete Search Landscape Analysis
**Date:** 2026-03-20
**Analyst:** Max (Senior Search Strategist)
**Client:** Sales Transformation Group (STG)

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Across 45 target queries spanning 6 categories, STG (salestransformationgroup.com) appears in the top 10 organic results for only **2 out of 45 queries** — both branded or semi-branded. STG is effectively **invisible** for the vast majority of high-intent commercial and informational queries that its ideal customers are searching.

The competitive landscape is dominated by:
- **Content/SaaS platforms** (ServiceTitan, HookAgency, JobNimbus, Roofr) who publish SEO-optimized guides
- **Niche training competitors** (Roof Sales Mastery, Top Rep Training, The Roof Strategist, FPG/Warrior Selling)
- **Industry media** (Roofing Contractor magazine, RoofersCoffeeShop)

STG's biggest opportunity lies in the **problem-aware** and **role-specific** categories where competition is thinnest and buyer intent is highest.

STG cannot compete head-to-head on tactical content volume — instead, STG should compete on **strategic/leadership content** (scaling, team building, sales management).

Queries like "owner stuck doing all the sales" and "how to create a sales playbook for contractors" have ZERO training company results. These are bottom-of-funnel buyers describing STG's exact ICP pain points. Creating content for these queries would be the highest ROI SEO investment.

Nobody has created proper "vs" comparison content for contractor sales training. STG could intercept every evaluation-stage searcher by publishing transparent comparisons.
