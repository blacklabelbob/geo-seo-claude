**Does CG Roofing handle hurricane and storm damage insurance claims?**
Yes. CG Roofing documents storm damage with photo evidence, provides itemized scopes
that align with insurance adjuster requirements, and performs code-compliant repairs
and full replacements across Jacksonville, Orlando, and Tampa.

**How do I know if my roof has hurricane damage?**
Look for missing or lifted shingles, granule accumulation in gutters, water stains on
ceilings, and bent flashing. After any named storm, CG Roofing recommends a documented
professional inspection even if no damage is visible — concealed damage is the most
common cause of denied claims when reported late.
