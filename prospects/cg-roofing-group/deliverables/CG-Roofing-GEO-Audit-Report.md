## Findings by Category

### 1. AI Citability — 24/100 (Critical)
- Page copy is built for keyword matching, not question answering. AI engines cite
  self-contained, fact-dense passages of ~134–167 words that directly answer a question.
  Current service pages bury facts (certifications, response time, warranty terms) that
  would otherwise be quoted by AI engines.
- No FAQ content structured for extraction. Questions homeowners actually ask AI
  ("How much does a roof replacement cost in Jacksonville?", "How do I know if my roof
  has hurricane damage?") have no citable answers on the domain.

### 2. Brand Authority Signals — 41/100 (Poor)
- Brand mentions correlate **3x more strongly** with AI visibility than backlinks.
- CG Roofing has excellent third-party validation (BuildZoom top 1%, GAF, BBB) but near-zero
  presence on the platforms AI engines cite most for local services: Reddit, YouTube,
  Wikipedia-adjacent directories, and local press.
- No entity reconciliation: "CG Roofing Group, LLC" vs "CG Roofing & Waterproofing, LLC"
  appear as inconsistent entities across the web — AI engines treat them as uncertain matches
  and skip them.

### 3. Content Quality & E-E-A-T — 38/100 (Poor)
- No named expert content. Caleb Green's licensing and experience are exactly the
  experience signals AI engines look for — currently absent from page-level authorship.
- City pages are templated; AI engines deduplicate templated content and cite none of it.
- No first-party data, project galleries with specifics, or case studies — the content
  types AI answers quote for "best roofer" queries.

### 4. Technical Foundations — 52/100 (Moderate)
- Static HTML pages: fast, crawlable, SSR by default — a genuine advantage.
- robots.txt does not explicitly allow the 14+ AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot,
  PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, etc.). Default behavior varies by host — needs explicit allows.
- No llms.txt file (the emerging standard AI crawlers use to understand site structure).

### 5. Structured Data — 12/100 (Critical)
- No RoofingContractor / LocalBusiness JSON-LD detected on key pages.
- No FAQPage schema. No Person schema for Caleb Green. No Service schema per offering.
- This is the single fastest fix with the highest AI-recognition payoff.

### 6. Platform Optimization — 18/100 (Critical)
- Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews for the same
  query — each platform needs tailored signals. CG Roofing currently has none for either.
- Google Business Profile signals are not reinforced by on-site entity data, weakening
  AI Overview eligibility for "roofer near me" conversational queries.
